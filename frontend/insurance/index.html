Car insurance questions

What is insured declared value (IDV)?

The value used for theft or total loss, subject to the insurer’s policy terms.

What are deductibles?

The amounts you contribute towards an applicable claim. A voluntary deductible can increase that contribution.

How are recommendations made?

Confirmed requirements filter quotes. Your priorities help order the matching offers. You can review or change those preferences.
